# Промпты сезонных панорам

Все PNG созданы встроенным **imagegen** в режиме `stylized-concept`. Исходный референс для дневных сезонов — [`world-background-panorama.png`](../concepts/world-background-panorama.png). Ночные версии отредактированы из соответствующего дневного сезона. Погодные SVG созданы кодом в [`weather-overlay-generator.js`](weather-overlay-generator.js), без imagegen.

## Весна — `spring-day.png`

```text
Edit the provided ORIGINAL post-apocalyptic 3:1 flat 2D panorama into EARLY SPRING DAY. Preserve the exact horizon structure and major landmarks: left fractured ruin, central broken city and elevated road, road winding right, tiny distant government complex. Keep the same simple Layered Light game art style, polygon bands, muted slate and charcoal, calm sky, no texture. Change season visibly but subtly: melting snow in shaded patches, fresh light-green shoots and sparse new leaves, puddles, moist soil, mild clear light. This is a FAR BACKGROUND for playable ground and buildings. No characters, labels, UI, logos, watermark, photorealism or new landmarks.
```

## Лето — `summer-day.png`

```text
Edit the provided ORIGINAL post-apocalyptic 3:1 flat 2D panorama into HIGH SUMMER DAY. Preserve the horizon, exact major ruined landmarks, broken central highway, road winding right, and tiny distant government complex. Keep the same simple Layered Light game art style: large polygon shapes, two or three solid tones, restrained outlines, no realistic texture. Warm but desaturated sunny sky, dry grasses, slightly more established green shrubs and a few small trees along the ruined roadway; the ruins still dominate. Make seasonal contrast from spring and autumn clear without changing composition. This is a FAR BACKGROUND behind playable land. No characters, buildings added to foreground, UI, labels, logos, watermark or photorealism.
```

## Ночь — `spring-night.png`, `summer-night.png`, `autumn-night.png`, `winter-night.png`

Каждая из четырёх ночных версий получена одним и тем же промптом для соответствующего сезона и его дневного PNG. В фактических запросах названия сезонов в начале были написаны прописными буквами.

```text
Edit the provided ORIGINAL flat 2D post-apocalyptic panorama from {season} DAY into {season} NIGHT. Preserve the exact same 3:1 composition, horizon, ruined building silhouettes, winding road, far government complex, and the source image's distinctive {season} seasonal ground, plants, snow or leaves. Use the same simple Layered Light game style: large clean shapes, two or three muted tones per object, no painterly texture, no photorealism. Deep charcoal-blue night sky, small muted moon instead of sun, a few very tiny stars, restrained cool shadows; keep terrain and seasonal features legible enough for a side-view strategy game. Add only a few tiny distant amber points in ruin windows and the far complex; no bright light show. This is a FAR BACKGROUND behind playable surface buildings and characters. No rain or snow particles, no foreground props, no UI, no labels, no logo, no watermark.
```
